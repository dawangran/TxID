# Figure data manifest

Figure 1 combines an illustrative identity workflow and exon example with retained fixed-model results. Annotation comparison: annotation-release-v29-v49.json/totals. Matrix dimensions: identity-partitions.json/evaluations, sorted runs only. Input-order results from identity-partitions.json/order_comparisons remain in Supplementary Table S3 and are no longer a main-figure panel.

Numeric source inputs are exported to figures/source-data.json and source-data.tsv before rendering. Displayed counts, percentages, node totals and ribbon geometry are recorded in figure1-plot-data.json; individual transitions are also retained in figure1-annotation-transitions.tsv. Builder: reproducibility/build_artifacts.py; current drawing code: reproducibility/render_figure1_editorial.py. Outputs: figure1.png (600 dpi), figure1.svg, figure1.pdf and figure1.tif (1200 dpi, LZW compression), at 178 × 122 mm. No invented measurements, error bars or performance rankings are added.

Panel a depicts GTF/GFF3 models entering canonical structures constrained by a reference FASTA, followed by exact identifiers and registry mappings, catalogs and classifications. Annotation versions enter through a separate dashed classification path; they are not inputs to the exact-identity hash. The exon illustration uses 1-based closed intervals on the positive strand. Display models M1, M2 and M3 map to source JSON keys A, B and C. M2 changes only the outer ends relative to M1; M3 moves the upstream exon end from 200 to 201. The three tracks share one coordinate scale, with the donor change labelled directly and no enlarged inset. SCa/SCb and TFa/TFb/TFc denote equality classes, not literal public identifiers.

Panel b is a proportionate alluvial diagram of changed classifications only. Eight source-coloured ribbons represent 29,435 changed observations among 538,804 paired observations (5.463%) between GENCODE v29 and v49. Colour follows the v29 source category, and the same count-to-width scale applies at both endpoints. The left node totals are Novel in gene 14,891, New locus 11,267 and Ambiguous 3,277. The right totals are Ambiguous 15,026, Novel in gene 13,869, Known 448 and New locus 92. These are counts within the changed subset, not complete category totals in either annotation. The two largest transitions are labelled 14,517 and 10,732. “Novel in gene” abbreviates novel_in_known_gene; it is annotation-relative classification, not a permanent identifier component. Unchanged observations are omitted from the ribbons. Exact-form ID changes remain zero across all paired observations. The sixth v49 input was classified without database persistence.

Panel c uses four sorted-run binary sample-by-group matrix relations: TxID exact form, isoSeQL exact ends, gffcompare tracking and isoSeQL common junction. Their column counts are 467,819, 467,908, 379,407 and 364,032, respectively, with six sample rows each. Vertical bars encode column counts from a zero baseline, not occupied cells or abundance. No occupancy heatmap is generated from the aggregate counts. The relations define different grouping units; fewer columns do not demonstrate greater biological accuracy. The isoSeQL results include the disclosed count-insertion compatibility patch.

Definitions and methodological qualifications are carried by the figure caption rather than a text footer inside the artwork. Layout metadata are recorded in figure1-layout-check.json; the composition review is in plan/review/figure-composition-review.md. The preceding minimal figure and associated manuscript artifacts are preserved in reproducibility/figure1-before-composition-redesign-2026-09-22.zip. Earlier figure archives remain untouched. The dimensions and checks document the delivered artwork; they do not establish journal acceptance or final submission readiness.
